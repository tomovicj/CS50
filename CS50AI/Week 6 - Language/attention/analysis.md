# Analysis

## Layer 1, Head 4

Most tokens attend to the token directly before them. Sometimes a token also attends to the token two positions before it.

Example Sentences:
- Then I picked up a [MASK] from the table.
- We turned down a narrow lane and passed through a small [MASK].

## Layer 11, Head 10

Almost all tokens attend strongly to the period ".", and almost no token attends to [SEP].
Some tokens near the beginning of the sentence attend to themselves instead.

Example Sentences:
- Then I picked up a [MASK] from the table.
- We turned down a narrow lane and passed through a small [MASK].
